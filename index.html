Understanding IB Mathematics Challenges

IB Mathematics is renowned for its rigorous curriculum and analytical depth. Many students find it challenging to manage the abstract reasoning, problem-solving under time constraints, and the high expectations of Internal Assessments. Through structured instruction and a personalized learning approach, I help students confidently navigate these complexities and build lasting mathematical understanding.

IB Mathematics Courses

  • Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (AA) – Designed for students with a strong interest in pure mathematics, especially those aiming for university programs in math-heavy disciplines. Covers algebra, calculus, and formal mathematical reasoning.
  • Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation (AI) – Geared towards students interested in practical applications of mathematics. Emphasizes statistics, modeling, and the use of technology to solve problems.

Each course is available at both Standard Level (SL) and Higher Level (HL), and I provide expert tutoring for all variants, ensuring clarity, confidence, and high performance.
