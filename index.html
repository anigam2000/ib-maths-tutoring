IB Curriculum Structure (At a Glance)

The IB Mathematics curriculum offers two pathways at both SL and HL levels:

  • Analysis and Approaches (AA): Theoretical, calculus-intensive, ideal for engineering and math-heavy university paths.
  • Applications and Interpretation (AI): Practical, data-driven, ideal for business, economics, and social sciences.

Each pathway includes an Internal Assessment (IA), timed exams, and rigorous conceptual understanding.
